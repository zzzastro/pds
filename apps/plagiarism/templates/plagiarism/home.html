About CiteCat

How it works: CiteCat uses TF-IDF vectorization (n-gram range 1–3) with cosine similarity to compare your submission against a reference corpus of text pairs.

Important: Only content similar to the training data below can be detected. The model was trained on image caption pairs — short descriptions of photos labeled as plagiarised (1) or not (0). If you submit text about unrelated topics, it will likely show no match.

Dataset: 367,373 text pairs from the PDS dataset. Each line: text1 \t text2 \t label (0 = no plagiarism, 1 = plagiarism).

Thresholds:

  • ≥ 30% similarity → Plagiarism detected
  • 10% – 30% → Caution: some similarities
  • < 10% → Clean (no plagiarism)
